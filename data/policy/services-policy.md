# Harbor services policy

This is the only policy the Harbor agent is allowed to quote.

- A scope change at or above USD 5,000 requires coordinator approval in Teams before any client communication.
- The agent may draft the client update and must not send it.
- Northline's statement of work covers a discovery phase of 80 hours.
- Brightwater's statement of work covers a support retainer of 40 hours per month.
- Rate cards are visible to that account's team and to finance. Hourly rates are USD 180 for a consultant, USD 240 for a senior, and USD 320 for a principal. Brightwater has a 10 percent discount. After-hours work uses a 1.25 multiplier. The minimum engagement is 4 hours.
- The agent does not invent approval limits, personal data, or compensation data.
