# Delivery charter

Harbor Field Services needs coordinators to approve scope changes at or above USD 5,000 before anyone tells the client. Project managers need answers from their own statements of work only.

## Milestones

1. Rate service and eval set pass offline.
2. Dev environment has the Dataverse tables, the custom connector, and the scope-change flow.
3. The Teams agent answers the eval set and refuses to send mail.
4. Test and Prod receive a managed solution from GitHub.
5. The operating review shows failure rate, open approvals, eval pass rate, and spend.

## Out of scope for v1

Microsoft 365 Copilot licenses, fine-tuned models, and a custom web chat. Meeting recap and draft text stay in the Copilot adoption note.

## Acceptance

A Northline manager can retrieve the Northline discovery hours and cannot retrieve the Brightwater retainer. A repeated idempotency key does not open a second approval. A connector timeout lands in the dead-letter table and the operations channel.
